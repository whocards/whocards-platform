# Mission

What WhoCards questions serve. Quoted from the WhoCards mission page and `soul.md` so the skill works without a checkout.

## The mission

> In this age of disruption and complexity, when our differences are used to keep us separate, one of the most courageous things we can do is to show up for others with authenticity, vulnerability and openness.
>
> Whether or not we recognize it on a daily basis, we are all deeply interconnected. WhoCards' mission is to strengthen the authenticity of that interconnectedness, because we believe that a sense of honest homecoming to ourselves and each other has the power to spark a light strong enough to lead us out from anxiety and loneliness towards belonging and love.
>
> We believe that by speaking what's true for us and actively listening to others as they do the same, we can quiet the noise of the world and enter the warm hum of mutuality.
>
> Alchemy occurs: Two anxious "I"s morph quickly into a relaxed and delighted "We."

## What the product is

WhoCards is a conversation game for people who are actually together. Curated questions move a real table, whether friends, a family, or a team, past "What do you do?" toward "Who are you?". The product succeeds when the phone stops mattering: someone reads a question aloud, and the conversation that follows is the experience. The same questions also serve one person in solo reflection or journaling.

## The character

A quiet invitation into a meaningful conversation: warm, curious, direct, human. Intimate rather than corporate. Playful rather than gamified. Bold without becoming loud. The question is always the focus.

## What this means for a question

- It deepens the conversation at the table rather than performing for a screen.
- It respects the mixed table: bilingual, cross-generational, cross-cultural. Language should travel.
- It stays honest: no manufactured urgency, no overclaiming, no trick.
- It invites vulnerability without demanding it. Honest self-expression and active listening are the point, not a lesson or a verdict.
