# Paddle Signup Verification for No-Entity Users

## Requirements
- **Individual Account**: Must be selectable as first-class option
- **KYC Process**:
  - Name, address, government ID (passport/driver's license)
  - No tax-ID/SSN required for individuals
- **Hungary Payout Methods**:
  - Bank transfer (OTPB, CIB, etc.)
  - No e-wallet options for Hungary
- **Fees**: 0% for first $1000/month, then 5% after

## Agent Checklist
1. Verify 'Individual' option exists in signup
2. Capture KYC document types accepted
3. Confirm Hungary bank transfer options
4. Document any review/KYB wait times
5. Note fee schedule visibility

## Blockers
- [ ] Individual account option not available
- [ ] Hungary bank transfer not supported
- [ ] KYC requirements exceed individual eligibility

## Notes
- No tax-ID required for individuals
- Payouts processed within 3-5 business days
