# Workshop offering directions

Status: broad direction agreed with Avi; draft copy for comparison with Tamas and other reviewers. Pricing, duration, and detailed activities for the paid offer remain open.

## Paid B2B

Make space for people to understand each other and become more conscious of how AI is changing the way they work together.

Avi wants to sell the relational experience in its own right. Meaningful connection during the workshop is worthwhile. Greater understanding of colleagues' experiences, choices, and expectations can carry into working life; the workshop doesn't guarantee trust, resolve conflict, or require changed behaviour.

The current direction is an existing team, with some preparation to understand the organization's setting and aim. Honest sharing and attentive listening remain central. Process reflection and useful AI knowledge can emerge, without becoming the main reason for the workshop.

The 90-minute conference prototype remains separate. It brings together people who aren't an existing team and helps us learn about the experience. Its timing isn't a fixed specification for the paid B2B offer.

## Future B2C

Explore learning AI by making something personally meaningful, with sharing and connection helping people discover what they want to do and learn from one another. Practical capability and personal agency can be explicit outcomes here. Develop this offering after clarifying B2B, carrying the same human foundation into a different purpose.

## Copy pass informed by the question skill

Read the installed `whocards-questions` v0.1 skill, its mission reference, and its editorial calibration. Apply their guidance to the invitation on `/third-relationship-v2`; this is a landing-page copy pass, not a skill update or a generated set of Questions.

The final agreed headline is “Meaningful connection. Conscious relationships.” A small “AI at work” line above it supplies the context. Avi preferred this bolder direction to “Take time to hear each other”; keep the body copy from the skill-informed pass. The introduction retains meaningful connection and more conscious working relationships as the purpose, with AI as the subject of exploration.

- Describe people and their lived experiences, using concrete ways into the topic instead of repeating abstract relationship language.
- Let participants supply their own experience. Avoid assuming that colleagues haven't listened, that something is wrong, or that everyone feels the same way about AI.
- Welcome play and imagination alongside difficult or unresolved experiences. Keep discovery open without requiring a lesson or a change.
- Use Avi's actual birthday app story to convey the personal experience behind the offer. Keep sharing voluntary and the card encounter unrehearsed.
